Selected engagements

Selected financial work.

Examples from Mark Fackrell’s finance and operations leadership engagements.

Global, multi-entity professional services

10 weeks

18 months of unclosed books cleaned up.
Audit completed.

Eighteen months of unclosed books. A completed audit in 10 weeks.

A private equity-owned, global, multi-entity professional services firm with approximately $100 million in revenue faced 18 months of unclosed books, a badly implemented ERP system and unreliable legacy records. The prior personnel had departed, leaving a completely new accounting team to untangle the problems.

Mark assumed executive-level ownership of the recovery, setting priorities, directing the new team and driving the reconstruction and cleanup of the financial records. Within 10 weeks, the team cleared the accounting backlog and completed the audit. Mark also led the due diligence support.

Private equity-owned education business · Company sale

Financial recovery, a company sale and a year of operational leadership.

Mark stepped into a private equity-owned education business with its books in disarray, cleaned up the financial records and led the business through its sale to private equity at approximately $100 million in valuation.

He served for one year as interim CFO with significant operational responsibilities, leading across finance and business operations until a full-time CFO was onboarded.

Scope: Financial cleanup · Operational CFO · Sale leadership · Transition to permanent leadership

Solar infrastructure business

Financial and operational leadership through restructuring.

Mark carried significant operational responsibilities alongside financial leadership at a solar infrastructure business navigating Chapter 11. He installed 13-week cash governance and helped protect core operations through the restructuring.

Scope: Operational leadership · Cash governance · Restructuring

Nonprofit financial reporting

Making the financial picture clear and usable.

Prepared organization-wide budget-to-actual reports, income statements and balance sheets for grant reporting. Aligned reporting categories to approved budgets and tied reports to the underlying accounting records.

Scope: Financial statements · Budget reporting · Grant support

Accounting systems & reconciliation

Getting two systems to tell the same story.

Performed invoice-level reconciliations between accounting and billing systems. Isolated unmatched items, duplicate records, credit issues and amount differences to identify the causes of receivables discrepancies.

Scope: Accounts receivable · System reconciliation · Issue resolution
