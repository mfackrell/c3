Insight / Decision timing

When does the business
need more leadership?

Look at how decisions get made, how work gets finished and how much still depends on you.

Signals to act on

The gap is affecting the business.

  • You remain the bottleneck for key decisions.
  • Execution slows unless you push it.
  • You cannot rely on the close, cash forecast or financial reporting.
  • Managers escalate issues without taking ownership.
  • A leadership seat is vacant or underperforming.
  • A raise, acquisition, sale or restructuring is approaching.

Signs your structure is working

Ownership is already clear.

  • A senior leader owns execution from decision to result.
  • Decisions move without your constant involvement.
  • Reporting is accurate, consistent and useful.
  • The team has capacity for current priorities.
  • Transitions have clear owners and a workable plan.

Match the response to the problem.

A delayed reconciliation may need targeted accounting support. A finance function without clear ownership may need a controller or CFO. Start by identifying the gap before deciding how much support to bring in.
