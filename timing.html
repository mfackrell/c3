Decision Timing

When do you actually need a fractional executive?

Most companies are already late by the time they ask the question.

You need this NOW if:

  • You are still the bottleneck for key decisions
  • Execution slows unless you push it
  • You don’t fully trust your numbers or reporting
  • Managers escalate instead of owning outcomes
  • You’re preparing for growth, a raise, or a transaction

You can wait (for now) if:

  • A senior leader already owns execution end-to-end
  • Decisions move without you
  • Reporting is clean, consistent, and trusted
  • You’re not actively scaling or changing

Most companies don’t hire when the need appears. They hire when the pain becomes unavoidable.

That gap is where growth stalls, execution slips, and small issues become expensive ones.

If you recognized even 2–3 of the “now” signals, you’re already operating with a gap.
