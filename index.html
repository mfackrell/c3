Eighteen months of unclosed books. A completed audit in 10 weeks.

A private equity-owned, global, multi-entity professional services firm with approximately $100 million in revenue faced 18 months of unclosed books, a badly implemented ERP system and unreliable legacy records. The prior personnel had departed, leaving a completely new accounting team to untangle the problems.

Mark assumed executive-level ownership of the recovery, setting priorities, directing the new team and driving the reconstruction and cleanup of the financial records. Within 10 weeks, the team cleared the accounting backlog and completed the audit. Mark also led the due diligence support.

Common questions.

How soon can we meet?

We can meet within seven days for an initial assessment. We agree on the engagement start date as part of defining the scope.

How does an engagement work?

We agree on the responsibilities and scope. Support can mean an embedded executive, a complete accounting function, an extension of your team or a defined financial project.

There is no minimum commitment. We shape the engagement around the work you need.

Is this interim or permanent?

Executive support can bridge the transition to permanent leadership. Accounting and CFO support can cover ongoing needs or a specific task.

Our primary goal is to take ownership through the transition and help transfer responsibilities to your full-time employees.

What kinds of companies do you work with?

Our experience includes founder-led businesses, private equity-backed companies and nonprofit organizations. Industries include professional services, education, SaaS, manufacturing, construction and e-commerce.

How is the work priced?

Pricing is based on the scope of work required. There is no minimum commitment.

Is our conversation confidential?

Yes. We treat all conversations, inquiries and client work as confidential.

Do you provide executives outside finance?

Yes. Mark leads finance and operations; Eric leads growth, strategy and technology transformation; Ravi leads strategy, execution risk and commercial systems. We define the responsible executive and scope for each engagement.

How fast can you start?

We can meet within seven days for an initial assessment and respond to inquiries within one business day. We agree on the engagement start date after reviewing the scope, priorities and availability. For an urgent need, email Mark directly.

Do you work with lenders and PE sponsors?

Yes. Our finance work includes cash forecasting, financing support, lender and board reporting, and transaction diligence. Mark has led financial recovery and operations in private equity-owned businesses.

Can you handle covenant reporting?

Yes. We can prepare supporting schedules, reconcile the underlying financial information and coordinate reporting requirements with your leadership team and lenders. The work is defined around your financing agreements and reporting deadlines.

Discuss the situation directly with Mark.
