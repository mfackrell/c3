Infrastructure — Restructuring

Stabilizing a business through Chapter 11.

Situation

Infrastructure operator entered Chapter 11 with no single executive owning daily restructuring execution.

Constraint

Severe revenue shock created continuity risk and threatened covenant compliance.

Action

Assumed interim finance and operations ownership; installed 13-week cash governance, lender reporting rhythm, and contract-priority decision rules.

Outcome

Implemented cash controls and contract restructuring to protect core operations during restructuring.

Manufacturing — Turnaround

Right-sizing to a viable core business.

Situation

Legacy manufacturer lost core accounts rapidly and had no turnaround owner across finance and operations.

Constraint

Sales declined 90% while fixed overhead, inventory, and vendor terms still reflected the prior business size.

Action

Led a 12-week reset covering SKU rationalization, asset disposition, labor realignment, and weekly liquidity governance.

Outcome

Liquidated non-essential assets and right-sized operations to stabilize the business and preserve value.

Software — Growth performance

From stalled pipeline to consistent growth.

Situation

SaaS business had demand signals but no executive owner for revenue execution.

Constraint

No sales for three consecutive months triggered investor concern and internal confidence erosion.

Action

Stepped in as interim growth lead; reset ICP, stage definitions, pipeline reviews, and weekly sales-operating cadence.

Outcome

Built pipeline ownership, stage-gate discipline, and forecast accountability; revenue stabilized at $200K/month and later doubled.

Funded Product — Investor Comms

Clarifying marketing ROI for investors.

Situation

Funded product business needed board-level visibility on marketing efficiency before its next financing gate.

Constraint

Fragmented marketing ownership made performance attribution unreliable in investor updates.

Action

Took interim ownership of growth analytics, standardized attribution logic, and implemented monthly board reporting packs.

Outcome

Implemented a unified performance scoreboard linking spend to outcomes, giving the board clear ROI visibility.

B2B Procurement — Scale-up

From stalled launch to a growth platform.

Situation

B2B procurement platform launched a new offer without one accountable owner for commercialization.

Constraint

A new offering had shipped but produced no revenue after six months because ownership spanned multiple teams.

Action

Installed a single launch owner, unified delivery architecture, and ran milestone-based weekly commercialization reviews.

Outcome

Consolidated ownership under one operating leader and simplified delivery architecture; the line scaled to 100+ staff and 25% incremental revenue.
