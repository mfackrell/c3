How quickly can an engagement start?

Timing depends on the scope, the expertise required and availability. Use the gap review to discuss your deadline and immediate priorities so we can confirm a realistic start.

What does an engagement look like?

We agree on responsibilities, deliverables and working cadence around your business. Support can mean an embedded executive, ongoing accounting coverage or a defined financial project.

Is this interim or ongoing support?

Both. Executive engagements can bridge a transition or provide fractional leadership. Accounting and CFO services can continue on an ongoing basis or cover a specific task.

What types of companies do you work with?

Experience spans founder-led businesses, companies preparing for transactions and nonprofit organizations, across professional services, manufacturing, education, SaaS, construction and e-commerce.

How are engagements priced?

Pricing is scoped to the work, level of responsibility and time required. We first define what you need, then agree on the scope and commercial terms.

Can the initial conversation be confidential?

Raise any confidentiality requirements before sharing sensitive information. Use the booking link or contact form to arrange a conversation; avoid including sensitive financial records in an initial inquiry.
