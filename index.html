Client outcomes

Examples of integrated leadership and delivery in practice.

Each engagement is unique, but the pattern is consistent: align leadership, install clarity, and deliver against commitments.

Education — Transaction readiness

Financial overhaul for a $100M acquisition

Context

Disorganized records and unreliable reporting slowed decisions and undermined buyer confidence during a strategic sale process.

Outcome

Delivered audit‑ready financials, forecasting, and diligence support to enable a successful $100M private equity transaction.

Cloud & infrastructure — Restructuring

Stabilizing operations through Chapter 11

Context

Severe revenue shock created continuity risk and threatened covenant compliance across lenders and key partners.

Outcome

Implemented cash controls, covenant tracking, contract restructuring, and disciplined workstreams to protect operations during restructuring.

Manufacturing — Turnaround

From contraction to a viable core business

Context

Sales collapsed from ~$500K to ~$50K per month and the cost base was misaligned with the new reality.

Outcome

Negotiated exits, liquidated non‑essential assets, and right‑sized operations to stabilize the business and preserve value.

Software & services — Growth performance

From stalled sales to predictable growth

Context

No sales for three consecutive months created a growth stall and pressure from investors.

Outcome

Installed a performance management system with clear accountability, taking the business to consistent $200K/month in sales, then doubling within nine months.

Funded product — Investor communication

Marketing performance scoreboard for investors

Context

Scattered metrics and unclear accountability made it difficult for investors to assess the effectiveness of marketing spend.

Outcome

Built a clear scoreboard showing performance at 5–6% versus benchmark, providing data‑driven answers and eliminating speculation.

B2B procurement — Scale‑up

From stalled launch to a major revenue engine

Context

A new solution launched after six months of effort with zero revenue, unclear ownership, and excess complexity.

Outcome

Simplified architecture and incentives; within two years the initiative became a 100+ person team and a vertical contributing more than 25% incremental revenue.
