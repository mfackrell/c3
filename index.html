Quick check

Does your company currently have an ownership gap?

Answer yes or no instinctively — no analysis.

1. Important decisions regularly wait for one specific person

2. Different leaders believe they own the same outcome

3. Meetings clarify problems but don’t resolve them

4. A major initiative exists without a single accountable executive

5. Hiring a senior role feels necessary but undefined

If 2+ are true: you likely have an ownership gap.
If 4+ are true: delay is probably costing money.
