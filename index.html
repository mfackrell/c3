When we are engaged

Situations where ownership matters most.

  • A critical executive seat is vacant
  • The business has outgrown the leader in the role
  • Private equity ownership or a change of control
  • Preparing for a transaction or audit
  • Turnaround, restructuring or Chapter 11
  • Execution slows after decisions are made

Our perspective

A filled seat is not the same as an owned one.

When execution slows after decisions are made, adding headcount rarely resolves it. The seat needs someone who owns the outcome. In revenue, that means taking responsibility for pricing, pipeline accountability and the operating reviews that move work forward.

In operations, it means turning decisions into clear priorities and carrying the work through. Our executives join the leadership team, assume responsibility for the function and execute through the transition to the right long-term leader.

Common questions.

How soon can we meet?

We can meet within seven days for an initial assessment. We agree on the engagement start date as part of defining the scope.

How does an engagement work?

We agree on the responsibilities and scope. Support can mean an embedded executive, a complete accounting function, an extension of your team or a defined financial project.

There is no minimum commitment. We shape the engagement around the work you need.

Is this interim or permanent?

Executive support can bridge the transition to permanent leadership. Accounting and CFO support can cover ongoing needs or a specific task.

Our primary goal is to take ownership through the transition and help transfer responsibilities to your full-time employees.

What kinds of companies do you work with?

Our experience includes founder-led businesses, private equity-backed companies and nonprofit organizations. Industries include professional services, education, SaaS, manufacturing, construction and e-commerce.

How is the work priced?

Pricing is based on the scope of work required. There is no minimum commitment.

Is our conversation confidential?

Do you provide executives outside finance?

Yes. We provide interim and fractional leadership across operations, strategy, growth and marketing, transformation, people and culture, and risk and governance, as well as finance.
