Client outcomes

What Fractional & Interim CXO leadership has delivered.

Representative situations where an interim or fractional CXO changed the outcome—by stabilizing leadership, accelerending decisions, and preparing the ground for a strong permanent hire.

Education — Transaction readiness

Preparing a $100M acquisition for scrutiny.

Context

Disorganized records and inconsistent reporting slowed decisions and raised concerns during a strategic sale process.

Outcome

Delivered audit‑ready financials, defensible forecasts, and diligence support to enable a successful $100M private equity transaction.

Cloud & infrastructure — Restructuring

Stabilizing a business through Chapter 11.

Context

Severe revenue shock created continuity risk and threatened covenant compliance across key relationships.

Outcome

Implemented cash controls, covenant tracking, and contract restructuring to protect core operations during restructuring.

Manufacturing — Turnaround

Right‑sizing to a viable core business.

Context

Sales declined from ~$500K to ~$50K per month and the cost base no longer reflected the size of the business.

Outcome

Negotiated exits, liquidated non‑essential assets, and right‑sized operations to stabilize the business and preserve value.

Software & services — Growth performance

From stalled pipeline to consistent growth.

Context

No sales for three consecutive months created a severe growth stall and investor pressure.

Outcome

Implemented a performance management system with clear accountability, leading to consistent $200K/month in sales and subsequent doubling within nine months.

Funded product — Investor communication

Clarifying marketing performance for investors.

Context

Disparate metrics and unclear accountability made it difficult for investors to understand the impact of marketing spend.

Outcome

Built a clear performance scoreboard showing 5–6% benchmark performance, providing data‑driven answers and ending speculation.

B2B procurement — Scale‑up

From stalled launch to a growth platform.

Context

A new solution launched after six months of effort with zero revenue and unclear ownership.

Outcome

Simplified architecture and incentives; within two years the initiative became a 100+ person vertical contributing over 25% incremental revenue.
